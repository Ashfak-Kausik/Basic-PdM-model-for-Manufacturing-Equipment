OR-Logic's recall is significantly higher than Traditional Voting across the 30 stability seeds (paired Wilcoxon p = 1.7e-6; mean +30.8 pp recall, OR-Logic higher in 30/30 seeds) — a large, unambiguous effect. OR-Logic's recall is also significantly higher than GBC (paired Wilcoxon p = 2.5e-6), but the margin is much smaller (mean +3.8 pp recall, OR-Logic higher in 29/30 seeds, 1 tie) — real and consistent, but a marginal practical gain rather than a wash.

This paired, per-seed test (`paired_tests.csv`) supersedes the single-split McNemar test in `mcnemar_results.csv` as the basis for the paper's recall claim: it directly measures the recall/FNR trade-off the OR-Logic method is designed to make, across 30 resamples, instead of a single-split test of overall correctness that weights false positives and false negatives equally.
